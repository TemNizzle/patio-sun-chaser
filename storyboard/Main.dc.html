Patio Sun Chaser
Validation video · positioning
Every patio list
tells you where.
This one tells
you when.
Sun is a moving target. The patio that's glorious at 2 pm is a shaded wind tunnel by 6. Every roundup in this city answers where — not one of them answers when. That gap is the entire product.
The claim Where vs. when Positions you against the 50-best listicle, not against other apps. The time slider is the proof no one else can show.
The feeling No bathing suit Sun isn't a vacation you book. It's a Tuesday, clothed, downtown, with a friend and a drink. Free and already here.
The urgency 7 weekends left Counted from today to Thanksgiving, Oct 12. Finite, spendable inventory — and it only gets smaller while you scroll.
What this video is testing
Do enough Torontonians care when the sun hits a patio to hand over an email address for it?
Real signal Email sign-ups per 100 link clicks.
Weak signal Likes. Saves and shares are worth more.
The A/B Cut A (claim) vs. Cut B (feeling), same CTA.
Trap to avoid: a feature tour. Sell the feeling first and last; the product earns the middle of the video, and one gesture — never a walkthrough.
