3Capture settings
Vertical, 1080×1920Everything shot 9:16 in camera. Never crop 16:9 down afterwards.
60 fps for the sliderThe drag reads as stuttery at 30. Screen-record at 60 and keep it there.
Lock exposure outdoorsShooting into the sun, auto-exposure will pump on every reframe.
Text in the safe zoneKeep captions clear of the bottom ~15% — the platform UI covers it.
4Build this before you post
Both cuts drive to a URL and ask for an email — and the app has no capture element today. Without it the video measures nothing, because clicks alone won't tell you whether anyone actually wants this.
—One field, email only, above the fold on the home view. Every extra field costs you conversions you can't afford at this volume.
—Make the promise concrete and matched to the video: “The Friday sun report — which patios catch golden hour this weekend.”
—Tag the inbound link per cut (?v=a / ?v=b) and store it with the signup, or the A/B tells you nothing.
—Set NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL and get a domain before posting — a raw vercel.app link in a caption reads as unfinished.
5Reading the A/B
—
Post 48–72 hours apart, same weekday, same hourTwo cuts on the same evening cannibalise each other and you learn nothing from either.
—
Judge on sign-ups per 100 link clicksViews measure the thumbnail and the algorithm. Sign-ups measure the idea — that's the number that decides whether this is a business.
—
Watch retention where the product appearsCut A hands over at 0:03, Cut B at 0:07. A retention cliff on either timestamp means the demo is the problem, not the hook.
